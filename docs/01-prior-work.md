## What has been counted before, stated precisely

- **Counted by NCERT:** nothing in words. The official booklets list chapters, page numbers and topic names per book.
- **Counted by journalists:** the number of textbooks read (Indian Express: 21 social-science books, 9 history books) and page counts for individual passages. Selected passages are quoted.
- **Counted by academics:** no study found that measures removed text across editions.
- **Not found anywhere in this search:** (a) words removed per chapter or per book; (b) the share of each book removed; (c) a systematic check of the official list against the printed books; (d) changes made in reprints after 2023 measured the same way; (e) a baseline rate of change between ordinary reprints; (f) public code and file hashes that let a reader reproduce the numbers.

## An honest contribution statement (for Sean to put in his own words)

- The paper does not discover that deletions happened, nor which famous passages went. Reporters established that in 2022-23.
- Its claim is narrower: a reproducible measurement of how much text changed, where, how closely the official list matches the books, and how reprints before and after compare.
- "First" should be written as "no such measurement was found in a search of Crossref and the press on 13 Oct 2026", with the search limits above.
