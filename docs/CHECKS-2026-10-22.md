# Second checks and the number rule (22 Oct 2026)

Two hand-check sheets were filled against the PDF pages of both editions, with the pipeline's (first-pass) answer
visible, so the checks are not blind. The text-free results are in `checks/`:

- `checks/07-check-results-sheet1.csv`: 30 old-edition pages on which the pipeline found removed words on pages
  NCERT's lists do not name (drawn with probability proportional to those words, seed 20261021, 22 science and
  8 social science). Q1 = is the text really gone from the new edition; Q2 = is the page really absent from the lists.
  The last two columns give each page's unlisted words after the change below and the cause of each error.
- `checks/07-check-results-sheet2.csv`: the fixed validation sample of 100 sentence units (seed 20261017), with the
  checked label. The first-pass (AI, extracted-text) verdicts stay in `results/validation_fresh_summary.csv`.

Sheet text (passages from the books) is not released.

Of the 8 pages whose text was not really removed, 6 held only renumbered section, figure, equation or exercise
numbers or a page number printed inside the text area ("7.18 ..." became "6.18 ..."), and 2 were text-layer
artefacts (a table, doubled bold type). Four pages also had their printed page two too low (leph2 back matter); this
did not change whether they were listed.

## Change made: numbers as wildcards (`code/ncertdiff.py`, NUM_WILD)
A digit-only token now matches any digit-only token inside a five-word run, when the run holds at most two such
tokens; runs with more numbers (tables, answers, data) must still match exactly. `run_diff.py --exact-numbers`
restores the old behaviour. Three tests were added (renumbering is not removal; a deleted table of numbers is still
removed; a kept table is kept); 18 tests pass. The whole corpus, the alternative pairings, the K=4, K=8 and no-fuzzy
sensitivity runs, the page test, the aggregates, the threshold check and the cross-check were re-run.
`compare_official2.py` now also reports, per book, the listed and unlisted words by sentence label.

Cost of the rule: in dropped chapters, where nothing survives, it lowers the removed count by 2,723 words (0.7%),
an upper bound on the deleted text it wrongly treats as kept.

On the sampled pages the rule removed every counted word from 3 failed pages (U05, U14, U21) and most from 2 more
(U09, U10). Of the 27 pages that still carry unlisted words, 21 pass both questions: 77.8% (exact 95% CI 57.7-91.4%).
Applied to the 25,196 unlisted words: about 19,600 (14,500-23,000), i.e. a check-adjusted unlisted share of 12.0%
(8.9-14.1%) of the 163,592 words removed from surviving chapters (the denominator is not adjusted, since removals on
listed pages were not checked). A word-weighted ratio estimate on the same draws gives 83.1% (bootstrap 67.9-95.2%).

## Labels and the totals
The totals count unmatched words whatever the sentence label: 98.4% of removed words are in units labelled deleted,
1.4% reworded, 0.2% kept or moved (unlisted pages: 89.0%, 8.2%, 2.8%). The moved label feeds no total; no count by
label is reported.
